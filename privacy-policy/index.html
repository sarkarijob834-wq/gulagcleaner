Privacy Policy

Your PDF stays in your browser.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

PDF Ad Cleaner is designed to collect as little data as possible. The PDF tool runs in your browser and your documents are not uploaded to this site.

Your PDF Files

When you choose a PDF, your browser reads it and the tool builds the cleaned file in your device’s memory. We do not receive, store or inspect your files. Closing the tab discards anything you did not download.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time) for security and reliability. If you email us, we receive what you send.

Cookies And Analytics

The site does not currently set advertising cookies or run analytics. If we add them, we will update this page and explain your choices first.

Third-Party Content

The homepage shows a thumbnail image from YouTube’s image server, which means your browser contacts that server when the image loads. When you press play on the video, your browser loads the YouTube player from youtube-nocookie.com. Those services have their own privacy policies. The PDF libraries are hosted on this site and are not loaded from a third party.

Search Tools

We may use Google Search Console to understand how the site appears in search results. Outbound links lead to sites with their own policies.

Children

The site is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Because we hold almost no personal data, there is little to delete. Contact contact@gulag-cleaner.online about any request relating to information you sent us.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date when we do.
