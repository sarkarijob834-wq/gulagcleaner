The Main Approaches

Ad removal methods fall into five groups. They differ in effort, quality and what kinds of ads they can handle.

ApproachBest forLimits
Rebuild original pages (Gulag Cleaner engine)Files where study pages are wrapped by adsNeeds a recognised structure; may not work on every file
Page cleaner (this site)Full-page ads, covers, notice pages, small margin bannersCannot remove ads drawn inside a page
Desktop or online PDF editorManual removal of objects and pagesSlow; some online editors upload your file
Cropping toolBanners at the edgesHides rather than removes; can cut text
Paid ad-free downloadClean originals without effortCosts money; depends on the platform’s plans

Gulag Cleaner

Gulag Cleaner is an open-source project under the GPL-3.0 licence. According to its repository, it extracts the original file by manipulating the PDF’s internal structure rather than cropping, and it is distributed in Python, Rust and WebAssembly versions as well as a website. Visit the repository or website for current details. This site is independent and is not affiliated with it.

This Site’s Page Cleaner

The PDF cleaner here is simpler on purpose. You see every page, decide what goes, optionally trim margins and download a clean copy. Everything runs in your browser. It is a good first step and a good fallback when automatic methods fail.

PDF Editors

Full editors let you delete pages, crop and edit objects. They give control but require time, and some free online editors upload your file to a server, as the Adobe Acrobat Online video on our homepage shows. Check each tool’s privacy statement.

Paid Ad-Free Downloads

Some platforms sell ad-free downloads. It costs money, but you get an original file and the authors and platform are supported. For documents you need every week, it can be worth comparing prices.

How To Choose

  1. Open the PDF and look at the ads. Are they whole pages? Use the page cleaner.
  2. Are they banners on every page? Try a small margin trim, or a rebuild tool.
  3. Are they wrapped around each study page? Use a rebuild tool.
  4. Is the file sensitive? Use offline software.
